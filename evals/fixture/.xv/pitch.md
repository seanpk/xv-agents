# The pitch

## In one or two sentences

Pantry shows one household what food it has and what runs out soon, so less of it goes to waste. It is for the people who cook in that household.

## Why it exists

Without it, food is found at the back of the fridge after it has gone off, and the same thing gets bought twice.

## Who it is not for, and what it will never do

- It is not for shops, grocers, or anyone outside the household.
- It runs on the household's own machine. It never sends the household's data anywhere else: no accounts, no sync service, no partners, no analytics.
- It is not a shopping service. It does not order food.

## The page

The household adds what it buys, with a quantity and a date it runs out. It marks what it uses. It asks what runs out within a few days. Everything else is out of scope until the household asks for it.
