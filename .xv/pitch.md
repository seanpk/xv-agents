# The pitch

xv-agents makes it easy for a team or an individual to adopt Xtreme Vibing in a repo: to vibe with discipline.

<!-- Sean's first wording, kept for reuse: "xv-agents supports the adoption of Xtreme Vibing in a given repo. For a team, for an individual, xv-agents makes it easy to vibe with discipline." -->

The people's statement of what the system is for. An agent may tidy it; it may not write it. It changes when a second person agrees, and the change is written down in `.xv/decisions/`.

## The page

### Why it exists

xv-agents makes Xtreme Vibing executable and empirical, and so usable and shareable. Without it, each team that wanted to adopt XV would have to figure out how to do that, and sharing learnings between teams would be high friction.

### Who uses it

Software engineering teams adopt xv-agents to enact a structured transition from traditional software engineering to "agentic engineering." Non-technical people adopt xv-agents to create software they can use and improve over time with more effectiveness and confidence.

### Who it is not for, and what it will never do

xv-agents is not for people or projects that will not trust agents to write production code. It will never be a teapot.
