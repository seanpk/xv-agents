# Rules

Project facts the agents cannot infer: an odd command, a slow check, a convention that breaks the defaults. Every entry carries the miss it came from. At the two standing moments of a burst, before it and as it ends, `xv check entries` lists them; an entry whose miss no longer happens without it is deleted.

Each entry is a heading and these lines:

```
### <the fact, one line>
- added: <date> by <name>
- from: <slice id>, <the moment in the record>
- miss: <what went wrong without it>
- seen: [<repo>@<date>]
```

## Entries

### A change to a brief, a may-not list, a standing order, or a check gets a line in `CHANGELOG.md` under Unreleased, naming the issue and who decided
- added: 2026-10-26 by Sean Kennedy
- from: 2026-10-25-diff-count, -suite-red-quote, -answer-in-prompt, -init-tries-guesses, the guardian's packets
- miss: four of five makers changed a brief or an order and wrote no CHANGELOG line; binding decision 1 asks for one, and the maker does not read the picture
- seen: [xv-agents@2026-10-26]
