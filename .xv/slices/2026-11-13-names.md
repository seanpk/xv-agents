# Report, record and stop names say what they hold (#185)

## Scenario

Rename the report, record and stop names that do not say what they hold.

The renames, old to new:
- "point", for a numbered line of a scenario or an amendment, to "outcome"; the report key `item` to `outcome`; the check `missed_points` to `missed_outcomes`
- the stop `red_misses_point` to `report_misses_outcome`
- the packet and record field `scenario_miss` to `change_misses_scenario`
- the report and record field `old_tests_forced` to `old_tests_to_move`
- the forecast fields `shape` to `shape_breadth` and `breadth` to `shape_note`
- the report and record field `may_not_files` to `may_not_files_needed`
- `send_back`, `sent_back` and `sent_back_to` to `sendback` and `sendback_to`
- the guardian report's `moved_tests`, a count, to `moved_tests_count`
- the stop `accept_moves` to `moves_to_accept`
- the record field `amended_at_report` to `reports_before_amend`
- the forecast field `keys` to `rows`
- the check `red_joined` to `red_joined_unsafely`, and the stop it fires to `red_command_joined`
- the record field `set_by` to `originator`
- the stop `read_only_broken` to `read_only_changed`
- the red field `watch_first_green` to `watch_before_merge`

1. Each agent's brief shows the new names, and each agent's report is read by them.
2. The record, the packet, the checks and the standing orders use the new names.
3. The README, the skills, the picture and the stop questions use the new names and the word "outcome".
4. A record or packet written before the rename is still read: the forecaster's history, `xv retro counts`, `xv record show`, and an open slice that resumes.
5. The two stop checks at `tests/test_run.py:1461` and `:1468` look for `covered` with its backticks, so they do not match inside `not_covered`.
6. A line in CHANGELOG.md.

## May ignore
