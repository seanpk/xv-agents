## The scenario

The person who set the work going can open a pairing with the watch's own session at an open stop or a send-back (`xv pairing start`), and end it at a resume step (`xv pairing end --next maker|cleaner|guardian`, or `scenario` after an amendment, never `close`).
While a pairing is open, `xv step`, `xv run`, `xv slice rebase` and `xv record report` refuse on the slice.
Commits carry the prefix `pairing (<name> with <agent>):`, and commits without it are listed, not refused.
Tests written or changed in a pairing are not blind: the record, the packet and the guardian's prompt name them.
A pairing ends only when each changed old test has a move, which is recorded in the person's name.
The pair session's spend, cut to the pairing window through `--session <reader>:<id or file>`, is one `step: pairing` row and counts in the budget.
A stop for two people is answered first unless the repo runs solo.
Docs (point 7) and `EXPLORE-NOTES.md` not merging (point 9) are the guardian's to check, as Sean settled at the stop answered 2026-11-11T22:46.
