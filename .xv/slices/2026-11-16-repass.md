# A short guardian re-pass, and a forecast that prices rounds (#178)

## Scenario

A guardian re-pass is short, and a forecast prices rounds.

1. After a pairing or a send-back, the guardian's prompt carries its own earlier packet and the diff since that packet's head. The five checks stay.
2. `.xv/config.yml` may name a model for the guardian's re-pass; the first pass keeps the step's model.
3. A wall recommendation a person accepted after a guardian pass stays on the record after the next pass.
4. The forecaster's brief asks for the number of rounds (passes through the agents) it expects, with its reason, and the record keeps it next to the budget.
5. The guide's why line names a pairing or a send-back as the cause of a gap between the forecast and the spend.
6. The forecast includes the forecaster's own spend, so a budget set from it covers that spend.
7. A line in CHANGELOG.md.
