# Some stops ask for --settled when the answer moves the slice forward

## Scenario

Some stops ask for `--settled` when the answer moves the slice forward (#82).

1. When a setup fails (`setup_failed`) or a prototype stops, the work has not started yet, so any `--next` moves forward. No `--settled` is needed.
2. A budget stop raised before its step ran is the same: `--next <that step>` moves forward and needs no `--settled`.
3. `xv record stop` takes `--step`, and the tests use it. They no longer edit the record text to add a step.
4. A test runs the README's general answer line (`README.md:295`), not only its words.

### Amended 2026-10-29 by Sean Kennedy

At the guardian's accept_moves stop, Sean accepted the moved helper `stop_saved_at` and narrowed points 1 and 2:

The exceptions to `--settled` are only for stops raised before any work of their step started:
- a `setup_failed` stop saved at step `setup`, not one raised after the scenario step;
- a prototype stop other than `prototypes_ready`: a two-person pick keeps `settled`;
- a `budget_spent` stop raised before its step ran, not the one raised after the maker left the suite red.

`README.md:295`, the watch brief (`skills/watch/SKILL.md`) and the picture name these exceptions, and `CHANGELOG.md` has a line for them.
