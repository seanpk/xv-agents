## Findings for the originator

**A. The guide and the budget still disagree on rows written before the merge.** The scenario says `totals` "needs no special case" (`.xv/slices/2026-10-26-token-cap.md:34`). The special case is still there:

```
scripts/spend.py:168
        if "cache_read" in s:
            t["tokens"] += sum(int(s.get(k) or 0) for k in ("tokens_in", "tokens_out", "cache_write"))
        else:
            t["tokens"] += int(s.get("tokens") or 0)
```

The budget (`scripts/orders.py:107`) and the retro (`scripts/retro.py:64-67`) use this function, so they recount old rows. The guide's appender does not:

