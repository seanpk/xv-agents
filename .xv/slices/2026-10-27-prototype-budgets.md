# A prototype that runs out of budget comes back to a person

## Scenario

A prototype that runs out of budget comes back to a person for a decision. In Sean's words: "whenever a prototype runs out of budget, it should come back to you for a decision. you cannot pick anything if no prototype was built, but you should be able to stop the prototype exploration. I guess that is a choice of "none"". So: (1) When any variant stops at its budget, the others still run, and then the slice stops for the person who set the work going, naming each variant that ran out and what it spent. The choices are: raise that variant's budget (`xv record set <id> prototype_budgets ...`) and run `xv prototype start` again; pick one of the variants that finished; or pick `none`, which ends the prototype exploration. A variant that ran out cannot be picked. When all three ran out, `none` is the only pick. (2) A second `xv prototype start` runs only the variants that stopped at their budget, each against its raised budget, and keeps the variants that finished, with their worktrees, recordings and side-by-side entries. A variant whose budget was not raised is not run again, and the stop says so. (3) `xv prototype start` no longer raises its own copy of `prototype_budgets_not_set`; the rule in `scripts/orders.py` raises it, and fires before any variant runs. This changes a standing order; Sean decided it, solo, and it gets a CHANGELOG line. (seanpk/xv-agents#55)

Settled at the guardian's stop, 2026-10-27, Sean: (A) A variant that runs out of its budget during its run is the case this slice is for. Decide whether a variant ran out from its spend after its run, not only from the spend recorded before `xv prototype start` began (`scripts/prototypes.py:117-119`, `:174`). Such a variant brings the slice back to the person who set the work going, as one that was over before the start does: it is named in the stop with its spend, it cannot be picked even though it has a branch, and a second start runs it again once its budget is raised. Test it with a variant whose run itself passes its budget.

Settled at the guardian's second stop, 2026-10-27, Sean: (B) A variant has finished only when it has a recording and did not run out. A second `xv prototype start` reruns every variant that has not finished, including one that ran within its budget but left no recording, and keeps only the variants that finished. A start with a new `--axis` reruns all three on the new axis and keeps none. Test both. (C) The stop says a variant that ran out during its run "ran out of its budget during its run", and one that was already over at the start "was not run"; test the wording. (D) The person who set the work going answers the budget stop alone when the answer is a budget raise. The stop says that a pick, including `none`, is for two people unless the repo runs solo, and `pick` keeps its two-people rule. (Small) The stop says to answer it before running `xv prototype start` again. The log line says who the pick is for in the same terms as the stop.
