# Decisions

Short records of decisions two or more people made from a packet, with who took part. They bind later agents. `xv record answer` writes one when a stop for two people is answered. Write others by hand in the same shape:

```
---
date: <date>
slice: <slice id, or none>
who: ["<name>", "<name>"]
---

# <the question>

<the decision, in a few lines, and what it refuses>
```

Naming a case where one person may decide a change to an agent is itself a decision here.
